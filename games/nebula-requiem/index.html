The hitbox

Only the white core of your ship can be hit. The glowing hull around it is cosmetic, so thread bullets closer than looks safe.

Graze & flux

Bullets that pass within a hull-width charge the flux meter and feed your score. A full meter unlocks Overdrive: doubled fire rate and slowed enemy time.

Synaptic grafts

Shards from wrecks fill the experience bar. Each level offers three grafts; legendary ones are rare. Bosses always offer at least a rare graft.

Sectors

Four waves, then a guardian. Each fallen guardian adds a sector mutator. Clear all four guardians to begin an Ascension loop with harder physics.

Stardust

Gold motes and your final score convert to stardust. Spend it in the Hangar on permanent refits and new hulls.

Touch & gamepad

On touch screens, drag the left half to steer and the right half to aim. Gamepads use the left stick to move, the right stick to aim, A to dash, B for nova, X for overdrive.
